# Code of conduct

OpenChip follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep
discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory
language aren't welcome in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

To report a problem, email **info@openchip.tech**. Reports are read by the
maintainer and kept confidential.
